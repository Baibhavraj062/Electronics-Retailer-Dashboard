# Store Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 1. Project Title / Headline

**Store Sales Analysis Dashboard – Product Performance, Profitability & Sales Trend Analysis using Power BI**

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

This Power BI project analyzes retail store sales data to uncover valuable business insights related to product performance, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, discounts, and geographic sales distribution. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics and make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

The analysis focuses on identifying top and bottom-performing products, understanding sales trends over time, evaluating discount effectiveness, and comparing sales performance across different cities.

## 3. Tech Stack

📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard Development & Visualization
🔄**Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Transformation
🧮 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI and Measure Creation
🗄️ **Data Modeling** – Star Schema Implementation
📑 **Microsoft Excel** – Data Source Handling

## 4. Data Source

The dataset used in this project was obtained from **Kaggle**, a popular platform for data science and analytics datasets. The data contains retail sales transactions, product information, customer details, promotional activities, sales values, profits, and discounts.

Data Source: Kaggle Retail Sales Dataset

## 5. Features / Highlights

### Business Problem

Retail businesses often struggle to identify profitable products, understand sales patterns, and evaluate the effectiveness of discounts and promotions. This dashboard addresses these challenges by providing a centralized view of key business metrics.

### Key KPIs & Analysis

* Top 5 and Bottom 5 Products by Sales
* Top 5 and Bottom 5 Products by Profit
* Top 5 and Bottom 5 Products by Quantity Sold
* Sales Trend Analysis (Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly)
* Relationship between Sales and Profit
* Average Discount Analysis by Category
* Total Number of Orders
* Sales Distribution Across Different Cities

### Data Modeling

* Implemented a **Star Schema** for efficient reporting.
* Created relationships between Fact and Dimension tables.
* Optimized model performance using one-to-many relationships.

### Dashboard Features

* Interactive Filters and Slicers
* Dynamic KPI Cards
* Product Performance Analysis
* Profitability Analysis
* Geographic Sales Analysis
* Trend Analysis Over Time
* User-Friendly Business Dashboard

### Business Impact

This dashboard helps decision-makers:

* Identify high-performing and low-performing products.
* Track business growth trends over time.
* Understand the impact of discounts on sales and profit.
* Analyze city-wise sales performance.
* Support strategic business and marketing decisions using data.
